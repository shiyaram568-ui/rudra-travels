Privacy Policy

At RUDRA TRAVELS, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy will inform you as to how we look after your personal data when you visit our website and tell you about your privacy rights.

1. Data We Collect

When you request a cab booking or a fare estimate, we may collect the following information:

  • Identity Data (Name)
  • Contact Data (Phone number, Email)
  • Location Data (Pickup city, Destination)

2. How We Use Your Data

We only use your personal data when the law allows us to. Most commonly, we use your data to:

  • Process your cab booking request
  • Communicate with you regarding your trip (via WhatsApp or Phone call)
  • Improve our website and customer service

3. Google Analytics & Cookies

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our website. This involves the use of cookies to collect anonymous traffic data. We do not use this data to identify individual users.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to others. We only share necessary trip details with our verified drivers to execute your booking.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at Rudratravel72@gmail.com.
